Ibory Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version. Ibory has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing about you is ever sold. Playing on your own needs no account and keeps everything on your device. If you make an account, the server keeps what it needs for cloud saves, high scores, chat and alliances, and you can delete all of it at any time.

Who runs Ibory

Ibory is a free game made and run by one independent developer. Questions, requests or complaints about your data: email iboryfree@gmail.com, or message the admin on the Ibory Discord.

Playing without an account

Your game is saved in your browser's storage on your own device. It is never sent anywhere unless you choose to: by making an account, or by saving a file or copying a save code yourself.

Ibory never asks for your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or payment details.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare runs the game server and stores the data above. It may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its network.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game's files and sees your IP address when your device downloads them.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game's fonts, so your device asks Google's servers for them.
  • Giphy powers GIF search. Your search words are passed on through the Ibory server (not linked to you), and GIFs load from Giphy's servers.
  • Your phone's push service (on Android, Google's) delivers notifications you turn on.
  • Pictures other players link in chat may load from the site they are on.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

What other players can see

Your in-game name, profile picture and frame, hero pictures, high-score numbers, alliance, and everything you write in world or alliance chat. Private messages are seen by you, the other player and the admin.

Your choices

  • Delete your account in the game (Settings, Account and cloud save, Delete account) or on the web at iborygame.com/account. Everything listed above that is keyed to your account is removed straight away. The game on your device is not touched.
  • Leave the high scores, remove your pictures, or turn notifications off in the game at any time.
  • Block players so their private messages don't reach you, and report messages or players to the admin.
  • Ask for a copy of your cloud save, or for anything else about your data, using the contact above.

Children

Ibory isn't meant for children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, contact the admin and it will be deleted.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed and repeated wrong guesses are locked out. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and important changes are announced in the game's Discord.
