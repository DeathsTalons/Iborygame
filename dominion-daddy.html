Quick start

Dominion Daddy watches the game through screen sharing, so it's built for a PC browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) with the game open in another window. On a phone or tablet, or if you'd rather do it yourself, use Manual mode in Settings & backup: cities, barb fields and alarms still work, you just enter things by hand.

  1. Open Screen reader below and press Start capture, then pick the Dominion of Ages window.
  2. Press Draw next to City list and drag a box from the top of your first city tab to the bottom of the last. A card and a barb field appear for every tab, then a form asks for each city's name and coordinates.
  3. Draw the Construction, Barracks header and March list boxes the same way. That's it — timers and hits fill in by themselves.
  1. Press + City for each of your cities and click a name to rename it.
  2. On a city's barb field press Edit, enter the city's coordinates, then type a label (5 for a barbarian town) and click tiles to paint them.
  3. Click a 5 tile when you march at it: it turns amber for the 4-hour reset and flashes when the march is back (set the March time in Edit for that).
  4. Use the alarm row on a card for anything with a timer: builds, research, barracks queues.
Finish setup— name each city and enter its coordinates (open the city in game: they're shown next to its name)
Coordinates are needed before targets can be painted on a field.

Alarm durations: 3h20m, 45m, 1:30:00, 2d4h (a bare number is minutes). Build, research and barracks timers fill in from the screen reader below.

Click a 5 tile to mark it marched at; click again while it's cooling to undo. A tile flashing blue is a march that's back: click it to dismiss. Shift+click copies a tile's coordinates. Other labels (valleys) are just notes. Edit sets the city's coordinates, size and march time.
